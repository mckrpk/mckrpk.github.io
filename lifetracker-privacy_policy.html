Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-24

This privacy policy explains how the Life Tracker mobile app for Android and iOS (called "Życiomierz" in Polish, hereafter the "App") handles your data. The App is developed and provided free of charge by Michał Karpiuk, an individual developer based in Poland (hereafter "I" or "the Developer"), who is the controller of your personal data within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. The short version

  • You can use the App without an account. Everything you record then stays on your device.
  • If you sign in, your actions and events are synchronised to Google Firebase so you can use them on other devices.
  • The App reads photos from your gallery only to show you the ones taken during an event. Photos are never uploaded, copied or changed.
  • The App sends crash reports and basic usage statistics to Google Firebase. You can turn crash reports off in Settings.
  • There are no ads, no data sales and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and all synchronised data from within the App at any time.

2. What data the App processes

2.1 Data you record

Actions you define (their names, order, archived state and the time they were created or changed) and events you record (their type, start and end time, and any notes you write). Without an account this data is stored only in a database on your device. When you are signed in, it is also stored in Google Cloud Firestore and synchronised between your devices.

Notes are free text. Please do not put information in them that you do not want stored with the service providers listed below, such as health details you would rather keep only on your device.

2.2 Account data (only if you sign in)

You can sign in with Google, with Apple (on iOS) or with an e-mail address and password. The App then processes:

  • your e-mail address, and your name and profile photo address if the sign-in provider shares them;
  • a user identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication;
  • whether your e-mail address is verified, the date your account was created, the platform you use (Android or iOS) and the version of the App you last used.

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to the Developer.

2.3 Photos

If you grant access, the App reads the date and time of photos in your gallery to show the ones taken while an event was going on. The photos stay where they are: the App does not upload, copy, change or delete them, and nothing about them leaves your device. You can withdraw access in your device's system settings at any time.

2.4 Crash reports and usage statistics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports (the error, the state of the App at the time, device model, operating system version, App version and an installation identifier). You can switch this off with "Send crash reports" in the App's Settings.

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Performance Monitoring. They collect technical, pseudonymous information such as app opens, screens viewed, session length, app performance, device and operating system type, approximate country derived from the IP address, and an app instance identifier. This data is used only to understand how the App is used and to improve it. It is not used for advertising.

2.5 Data export

"Export data" creates a file with your data on your device. The App does not send that file anywhere; where it goes is up to you.

3. Why the data is processed (legal bases)

  • Storing and synchronising your records and running your account: to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Crash reports, usage statistics and performance data: the Developer's legitimate interest in keeping the App reliable and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time, for crash reports by switching them off in Settings.
  • Reading your photos: only after you grant the system permission.

4. Who else processes the data

The Developer does not sell or rent your data and does not share it with anyone for their own purposes. The App relies on these service providers, which process data on the Developer's behalf:

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd - Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Performance Monitoring (Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy);
  • Apple Inc. - Sign in with Apple, if you use it (Apple Privacy Policy).

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data may also be disclosed where the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from a public authority.

5. How long the data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the App, delete your account or uninstall the App.
  • Your account and synchronised data: until you delete your account. Deleting an action or an event in the App marks it as deleted so that the deletion reaches your other devices; it is removed for good together with your account.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days. Usage statistics: up to 14 months (Google's retention settings).

6. Deleting your account and data

In the App, open Settings and choose "Delete account". This deletes your Firebase account, all data synchronised to the cloud, and the data stored on the device.

If you no longer have the App installed, you can ask for deletion by e-mail at karpiuk.mich@gmail.com, sending the message from the address linked to your account. The account and all synchronised data will be deleted within 30 days.

If you never signed in, all your data is on your device only; uninstalling the App removes it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to data portability (you can use "Export data" or ask by e-mail). To use any of these rights, write to karpiuk.mich@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

8. Security

Data sent between the App and Firebase is encrypted in transit. Access to your synchronised data in Firestore is restricted to your own account by security rules. As the operator of the service, the Developer has administrative access to the database and uses it only to maintain the service or when you ask for help. No system is completely secure, but the Developer takes reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact the Developer and it will be deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the App changes. The current version is always available on this page, with its effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.
