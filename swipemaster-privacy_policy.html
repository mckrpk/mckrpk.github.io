Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Swipe Master Space game for iPhone and iPad (the "App"), created by Michał Karpiuk. The App is free and contains no ads.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any data to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Your level records, achievements and settings (such as music on/off and sensitivity) are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the App.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits your scores and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center so they appear in leaderboards and achievements. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center data. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

App Store ratings

The App may occasionally show Apple's standard rating prompt. Any rating you give is handled by Apple.

Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
