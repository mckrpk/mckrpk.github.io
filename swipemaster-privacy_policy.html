Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Swipe Master Space game for Android, iPhone and iPad (the "App"), created by Michał Karpiuk. The App is free and contains no ads.

Data we collect

The App has no accounts and does not ask you for any personal information such as your name or email address. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising. The services described below process some data on our behalf or on their own; nothing else is sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your level records, achievements and settings (such as music on/off and sensitivity) are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the App or clear its data.

Android: Google Play Games Services

On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, the App submits your scores, your local level records and your achievement progress to Google Play Games Services so they appear in leaderboards and achievements. Google links them to your Play Games profile (gamer name, avatar and player ID) and shows them according to your Play Games privacy settings. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We can see leaderboard entries and aggregate statistics in Google Play Console, but we do not receive your Google account email address. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and data in the Google Play Games settings.

Android: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

On Android, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used (for example app opens, session length and device model, linked to a random app instance identifier; the advertising ID is not collected) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (stack trace, device model, operating system version and a random installation identifier). This data is not linked to your name or account and is used only to improve the App, not for advertising. It is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

iPhone and iPad: Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits your scores and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center so they appear in leaderboards and achievements. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center data. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. The iPhone and iPad version has no analytics and no crash reporting.

Ratings

On iPhone and iPad, the App may occasionally show Apple's standard rating prompt. Any rating you give is handled by Apple.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information. Signing in to Google Play Games or Game Center is optional, and the game can be played without it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
